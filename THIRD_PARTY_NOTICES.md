# Third-party notices and licensing status

This repository is at an early research stage.

- **Apple XNU:** upstream source is maintained at https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/xnu. Review the exact license and preserve notices before importing or modifying any upstream file.
- **Other third-party code:** record the project, version or commit, source URL, license, and local modifications before inclusion.
- **Firmware:** proprietary firmware and device dumps must not be committed.

There is intentionally no blanket license grant for the repository at this time. Original project code licensing will be selected after reviewing compatibility with upstream and third-party licenses. Do not assume that a repository-level MIT, Apache-2.0, or GPL license applies to every component.
