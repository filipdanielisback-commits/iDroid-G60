# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, welcoming, and constructive project community.

- Be respectful and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions from beginners.
- Be honest about uncertainty and limitations.
- Do not publish private information or credentials.
- Do not harass, threaten, or discriminate against contributors.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these standards.
